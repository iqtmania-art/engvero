Editorial Policy & Methodology

1. Standards & CEFR Alignment

Lessons are categorized using Common European Framework of Reference (CEFR) descriptors (A1–C2) and cross-checked against standard learner dictionaries (Cambridge and Oxford references).

2. Authorship & Review

Maintained by: ENGVERO Maintainers & Curriculum Team. Every lesson is reviewed for naturalness, clear contrastive examples, and accurate IPA transcription before publication.

3. Corrections & Updates

Spot a typo or unclear example? Report it via our Contact page. Verified corrections are applied and reviewed regularly.
