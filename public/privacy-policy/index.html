Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

1. Speaking Practice & Microphone Audio

When you use the Speaking Practice recorder, audio is captured temporarily in your browser memory for self-playback only. ENGVERO does not upload, record, or store your voice recordings on external servers.

2. AI Tutor Chat

Messages submitted to the ENGVERO AI Tutor are transmitted to our AI model provider (Google Gemini API) solely to generate educational responses. Please do not enter sensitive personal, financial, or confidential information into the chat.

3. Local Progress Storage

Quiz scores or study preferences may be stored locally in your browser (localStorage) and remain under your control.

4. Contact & Inquiries

If you submit feedback via the Contact page, your message and email address are used only to respond to your inquiry.
